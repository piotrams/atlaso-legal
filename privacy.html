Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Atlaso is a geography trivia game. We don't run accounts, analytics or servers of our own. The only third party that receives data is Google, which serves ads to players on the free version. This page explains what that means.

What we collect

We (the developer) collect nothing. There are no accounts or sign-up, and we never receive your name, email or progress.

Advertising (free version)

The free version shows an occasional full-screen ad after a journey, served by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google's SDK may collect:

  • Device and advertising identifiers (including Apple's IDFA, only if you allow tracking)
  • Advertising data, such as which ads you saw or tapped
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address
  • Diagnostic and performance data about the ad SDK

This data goes to Google, not to us, and is handled under Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses data from partner apps.

Your choices. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent before ads are personalised, and you can change your choice any time in Atlaso under Profile → Settings → Ad privacy choices. On iOS, Atlaso asks permission before using your device's advertising identifier; you can change this in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. If you decline, you may still see ads, but they won't be personalised.

Atlaso Pro subscribers see no ads, and the ad SDK does not load ads for them.

What stays on your device

Your progress (countries and cities discovered, scores, streaks, settings) is stored locally on your device. It is not sent to us. Deleting the app removes it.

Game Center

If you choose to use leaderboards, Atlaso uses Apple Game Center. Game Center is operated by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. We only submit your score to Apple's leaderboards; we do not receive your name or other Game Center data on our own servers.

Atlaso Pro purchases

Subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. Apple tells the app whether a subscription is active, so we can unlock Pro features.

Children

Atlaso is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and we do not collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
